| `web/` | favicon.svg (light/dark), favicon.ico, 16/32 px PNG, apple-touch-icon, 192/512, maskable, site.webmanifest |
| `banners/` | LinkedIn 1584x396, X header 1500x500, Open Graph 1200x630 (SVG and PNG) |
| `brand-tokens.json`, `brand-tokens.css` | Color, type, radius, spacing, motion |
