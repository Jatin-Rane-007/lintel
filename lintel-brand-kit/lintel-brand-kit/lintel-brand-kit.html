Structure that lets the light in.

A lintel is the beam above a door or window. It carries the weight above an opening so people and light can pass through. This kit holds the logo system, color, type, app icon, favicon and banners for Lintel, a property discovery app.

Logo

The symbol is a thick lintel beam, two posts and one ember point of light in the opening. The wordmark is lowercase Poppins SemiBold with −2% tracking and an ember circle in place of the period, the sun-point. All logo files are outlined; never retype the wordmark.

Parts

Clear space and minimum size

Let u be the beam thickness of the symbol. Keep at least 2u clear on every side. Symbol: 16 px on screen, 6 mm in print. Horizontal lockup: 80 px or 20 mm wide. Stacked lockup: 48 px or 14 mm wide. Below 32 px, use the small symbol with the heavier beam and larger point.

Please don’t

Stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows or gradients. Recolor outside the palette. Move the ember point or swap it for another shape. Add a second beam, which turns the mark into a torii gate. Round the corners. Set the wordmark in uppercase, or in Ember (it fails contrast). Place the logo on a busy photo without a solid Paper chip or a dark scrim. Without its point, the symbol reads as the letter π, so always include it.

In running text, write “Lintel” with a capital L and no symbol.

Verified contrast

Ratios are computed with the WCAG relative-luminance formula. Ember, Sky, Moss and Stone are fills and shapes, never text on Paper. Ember on Paper and Paper on Ember fall just under 3:1, so don’t rely on them alone for required UI indicators; add a second cue such as shape or an outline.

Type

Poppins sets headings, numbers, labels and buttons (400, 500, 600). Lora sets reading text (400 and italic). Fallbacks: Poppins, “Helvetica Neue”, Arial, sans-serif; Lora, Georgia, “Times New Roman”, serif.

App icon and favicon

Android adaptive icon

Ink background, Paper structure, Ember point, with the symbol inside the central 66 dp safe zone of the 108 dp canvas. A monochrome layer supports themed icons. The previews show circle, squircle, rounded-square and themed masks.

Banners

Paper ground, the horizontal lockup, the tagline in Poppins SemiBold and a 2–3 px Ember sun-line. Content stays centered so profile photos don’t cover it. The PNG exports have the type outlined.

Voice

Warm, precise, unhurried and plain. Sentence case throughout. No exclamation marks, superlatives or urgency tricks. Every figure carries a date or source, and calculated values are labeled “Estimate”. We don’t invent testimonials, ratings, statistics or awards.

Tagline: “Find a home with good light.” Alternates: “Homes, in good light.” and “Look at homes the way the day does.”
